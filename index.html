The research

PhD thesis: Bicyclo[1.1.1]pentane Analogues of Quinolone Antibiotics, supervised by Dr Marc Kimber at Loughborough University.

1

The problem

Quinolones are among the most widely used antibiotic classes, but resistance to them keeps rising. Antimicrobial resistance is one of the biggest threats in modern medicine, and new chemical starting points are needed.

2

The idea

Many drugs are built from flat aromatic rings. Bicyclo[1.1.1]pentane (BCP) is a compact, rigid cage that can stand in for those flat pieces as a bioisostere, adding three dimensional shape that can change how a molecule behaves in the body.

3

The chemistry

Strained cages are hard to attach. Dolapo develops multistep routes to N-BCP quinolin-4(1H)-ones through masked quinolinium intermediates, and extends them to fluorinated and 3-aryl substituted analogues for structure activity studies.

Research journey

From materials and environmental chemistry in Nigeria to medicinal chemistry in the UK.

2024 to present

PhD Chemistry (Organic Chemistry)

Loughborough University, UK · Fully funded studentship

Thesis: Bicyclo[1.1.1]pentane Analogues of Quinolone Antibiotics. Supervisor: Dr Marc Kimber

  • Design and synthesise BCP containing quinolone analogues as three dimensional bioisosteres for antibiotic discovery.
  • Develop and optimise multistep routes through quinoline and quinolinium systems and functionalised BCP scaffolds.
  • Study reaction conditions, scope and mechanism to improve efficiency and selectivity.
  • Characterise new compounds by 1D/2D NMR, HRMS, IR, HPLC and GC, working under inert atmosphere with Schlenk and glovebox techniques.
  • Demonstrate in undergraduate organic chemistry labs and support MSc research students.
2023 to 2024

MSc Chemistry

Nottingham Trent University, UK

Thesis: Microfluidic Mixing Analysis of Liquid Systems. Supervisor: Dr Cheu Wing Li

  • Designed and analysed microfluidic T junction systems to study mixing, diffusion and flow.
  • Used quantitative analysis and experimental optimisation to evaluate mixing performance.
2018 to 2020

  • Synthesised and characterised starch based nanoparticles for removing Pb(II) and Cd(II) from water, published in 2021.
  • Modelled adsorption with kinetic, thermodynamic and isotherm models.
